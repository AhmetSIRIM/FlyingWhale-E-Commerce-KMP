# Working conventions

## Mainstream by default

Reach for what the tool already ships: presets, defaults, documented paths. A
local reimplementation of something upstream provides still has to be maintained
here, and it drifts from the thing it copied.

Deviating is allowed and sometimes right. State the deviation and the reason for
it before writing it, not after.

## Issues and pull requests state, they do not narrate

Context in a sentence or two, then the decision and what it changes. Leave out
how the problem was found, and leave out anything the diff already shows.
